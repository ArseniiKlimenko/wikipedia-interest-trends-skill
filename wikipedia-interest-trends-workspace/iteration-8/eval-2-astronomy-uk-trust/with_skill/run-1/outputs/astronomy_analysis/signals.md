How to read:
- Agreement: both signals moving the same way makes the conclusion much stronger. DISAGREE often means readers moved from Wikipedia to search/AI answers (Trends up, Wikipedia down) or a Wikipedia-specific effect; say so instead of picking one.
- Google Trends is an index relative to its own peak (0-100); compare directions, not levels, across countries.
- App Store numbers describe competition: many rated apps = proven demand but crowded; few recent entrants with a large rating share = hard to break in; many new apps gaining ratings = open, moving market.
- The App Store API has no search volume; keyword results depend on the exact wording (try the local phrase).
